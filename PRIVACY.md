## Without an account
Everything stays on your device: settings, plays, tutorial progress and training history. We don't collect it.

## With an account
A **parent, guardian or coach** (or a player 13 or older) creates the account. We store:
- the account **email address** and a securely hashed **password** (handled by our sign-in provider)
- for each player: a **nickname** and an optional **age group** (like "12U"). We ask for nicknames, not real names, and never ask kids for email addresses, birthdays, photos or locations
- each player's **training data**: rep results, coverage-recognition answers, plays they've built, tutorial progress and app settings

We use this only to sync progress between your devices and to show training stats inside the app.

## What we don't do
No ads. No selling or sharing of personal information. No chat or messaging between users. No tracking across other apps or websites.

## Where it's stored
In our database provider (Supabase), protected so that each account can only access its own players.

## Your choices
- **Download** all your data: Settings → Account & Players → Download my data.
- **Delete** a player, or the whole account and all its data: Settings → Account & Players. Deletion is permanent.
- Parents can contact us at [contact email] to review or delete a child's information.

## Children
Accounts are for adults (or players 13+). Younger players use a nickname profile that their parent or guardian creates and controls.
