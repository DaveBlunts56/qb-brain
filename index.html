      HOW THE SCORE WORKS
      Every session with 5+ reps gets a QB Brain Score from 0–100, built only from what the game measures on each rep:
      • Decisions 45% — plays where you made the right read without a turnover-worthy throw
      • Coverage ID 25% — coverages you named correctly when asked
      • Speed 15% — release time: 1.4s or quicker is full marks, 3.2s or a sack is none
      • Ball security 15% — each 1% of throws that were turnover-worthy costs 3 points
      Parts a mode doesn't measure are left out. Then difficulty: Rookie ×0.88, Varsity ×0.95, Elite ×1. Your rating is the average of your last 10 scored sessions, and it sets your rank. Captain and Field General need regular Varsity or Elite sessions; Franchise QB needs Elite.
